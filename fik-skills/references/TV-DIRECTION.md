# FIK TV — art direction
Design read: cinematic PERSONAL TV control surface, viewed from across the room, constrained by low-memory Fire OS 7 and limited D-pad input.
Tone: calm, expensive, intentional. No sales slogans, fake editorial copy, floating particle effects, infinite content recommendations or glass everywhere.
Mechanisms drawn from tvOS HIG: content first, focus enlarges, visual depth from contrast and one backdrop, spacious top navigation, content-led immersive panel. Not a tvOS clone, and no Apple assets.
FIK Apple mode: blue `#0066cc` is sole UI accent; 400/600/700 weights; 1px hairline boundaries; functional pills; no unnecessary card drop shadows.
One strong composition: hero introduces the focused INSTALLED APP; its actual icon serves as the object in the hero. Everything else recedes.
Use direct natural product copy: selected app's real name and `Press OK to open`. Avoid fabricated media content.
Tuning: design variance 5/10, motion 3/10, density 4/10, prioritize 60fps focus, no autoplay or remote-dependent online requests.
